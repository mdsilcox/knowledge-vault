## What went well
- Seeding through the real tools exercised the hook 24 times with no failures; the only hook messages were forward links to notes written later in the batch.
- All five skill scenarios passed in a fresh session in another project, including edit-instead-of-duplicate (naming the contributing project and setting `updated`) and asking before deleting.

## What to watch
- **Tuned evaluation questions overstate quality.** The seed lane reworded its gate questions until each ranked first. Five questions written without looking at results put the right note first 3 times in 5 (hybrid) and in the top 3 4 times in 5. The skill now scans the top 3-5 titles, and the gate includes the untuned measure. Revisit ranking (or a bigger embedding model) as the vault grows.
- Broad notes (the survey, decisions) attract matches in both keyword and vector search.
- The per-session fixed cost is still an estimate; measure it with `/context` some day.

## Change next time
- Write evaluation questions before seeing any results, and keep them separate from anything an agent may tune.
- Brief lanes that write evaluation data to say so explicitly: questions must not be reworded to pass.
